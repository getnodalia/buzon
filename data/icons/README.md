# Buzón app icon

Nodalia app icons are black-and-white portraits of historical figures over the app colour.
Buzón's is Sir Rowland Hill, who brought in the Penny Post, on its red (`#D4241B`).

Everything here is generated from `assets/app-icon/buzon-art.png` by `tools/icon-svg.py`; the
figure, the source photograph and its licence are in
[assets/app-icon/README.md](../../assets/app-icon/README.md).
