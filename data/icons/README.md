# Buzón app icon

Nodalia app icons are black-and-white portraits of historical figures over the app colour.
Buzón's tile uses its accent, `#D4241B`.

`dev.danielmiguelt.Buzon.png` is the 192 px tile shown on [getnodalia.com](https://getnodalia.com).
The full-resolution master, the hicolor sizes (16–512 px and scalable), the Linux app-grid variant
made with `tools/nodalia-app-icon.py` from
[nodalix-icon-theme](https://github.com/danielmigueltejedor/nodalix-icon-theme), and the macOS
`.icns` arrive with the packaging.
